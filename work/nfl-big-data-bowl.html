NFL Big Data Bowl 2026

A Kaggle competition entry in the prediction track: forecasting football play outcomes before they happen, from player-tracking data.

Type
Kaggle competition
Field
Sports analytics
Data
650K+ tracking records
Tools
PyTorch, GroupKFold

Problem

Predict play outcomes before they occur using Next Gen Stats player-tracking data, without letting future information leak into the model.

What I built

I preprocessed more than 650K tracking records and implemented deep neural networks in PyTorch. I used GroupKFold cross-validation so that related records never sit on both sides of a split.

Outcome

A leakage-free predictive pipeline that forecasts play outcomes before the snap, validated on grouped splits of real NFL tracking data.
