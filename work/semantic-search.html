Feedback and semantic search platform

A serverless platform that finds the right customer feedback by meaning, not by matching keywords.

Type
Serverless AI project
Field
Vector search
Data
1K+ feedback records
Tools
Cloudflare Workers, Vectorize

Problem

Manual keyword searches could not surface the right customer feedback, because the words people search with rarely match the words in the records.

What I built

A fully serverless semantic search platform on Cloudflare Workers and Vectorize. It uses embeddings to ingest and query more than 1K customer feedback records in real time.

Outcome

Semantic matching replaced manual keyword searches and answers queries in under 100ms.
