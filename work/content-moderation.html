AI content moderation system

An end-to-end machine learning pipeline that catches harmful and spam content quickly, and keeps people in control of the edge cases.

Type
Machine learning project
Field
Content safety
Speed
Under 70ms per item
Tools
Python, scikit-learn, NLP

Problem

Harmful, spam and threat content needs to be caught automatically at scale, fast enough not to slow the product down, with humans still deciding the edge cases.

What I built

A moderation pipeline in Python: a multi-label text classifier using scikit-learn and NLP feature engineering, combined with a human-in-the-loop review architecture.

Outcome

The classifier detects harmful and spam content with high accuracy while staying under 70ms per item.
