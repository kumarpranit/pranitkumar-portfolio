NFL Big Data Bowl 2026 – Prediction Track

Kaggle Competition | Sports Analytics & Predictive Modeling
650K+ tracking recordsPyTorch deep learningGroupKFold no-leakage validation
Problem

Predict football play outcomes before they occur, using Next Gen Stats player-tracking data — without letting future information leak into the model.

What I Built

Preprocessed 650K+ tracking records and implemented deep neural networks in PyTorch, with GroupKFold cross-validation to prevent data leakage.

Outcome

A leakage-free predictive pipeline that forecasts play outcomes pre-snap, validated on grouped splits of real NFL tracking data.
